03

Buttons

One shape language (full pill), one shadow recipe, three jobs: commit, reconsider, flag.

Primary = teal fill + soft lifted shadow. Secondary = teal outline, transparent fill. Coral is reserved for things awaiting the user's action — never a routine confirm.

05

Signature mark

One custom glyph, used everywhere the generic handshake/heart clip-art used to live: two paths crossing and completing each other — literally, a swap.

This exchange glyph replaces the stock handshake and heart-in-hand icons on the success and empty states. It's simple enough to sit at 18px inside a card, and expressive enough to anchor a full-screen "Request sent" moment.

Use it in outline form on light backgrounds, and knock it out in cream on teal or coral fills. No other icon competes with it for "brand" duty — everything else in the icon set (chat, pin, star, clock) stays a plain, quiet line icon.
